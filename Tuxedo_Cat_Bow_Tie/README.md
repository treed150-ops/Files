# Tuxedo Cat Book with Bow Tie

Updated tuxedo cat with a raised black bow tie on the white chest bib. Two rounded wings and a raised center knot form one connected detail. The black tie extends into the underlying black body, with the white material fitted around it.

Dimensions: 94.624 × 59.178 × 13.600 mm, unchanged. Bow wings reach Z=13.35 mm and the knot Z=13.60 mm. Original flat bottom, body, tail, face, center opening, and tail hole are preserved. No original model volume was removed.

## Bambu Studio

Open `Tuxedo_Cat_Bow_Tie_Book.3mf` and assign black and white filaments to the matching named parts. This is a portable model assembly; manual filament assignment may be needed. It contains no printer-specific slicing settings.

Alternatively select `Tuxedo_Cat_Bow_Tie_Black.stl` and `Tuxedo_Cat_Bow_Tie_White.stl` together, import as one object with multiple parts, preserve their relative coordinates, and assign the two filaments. Do not place the white part independently on the bed. Its five closed islands are supported by the black body during printing.

`Tuxedo_Cat_Bow_Tie_Book.stl` is the complete single-solid version. STL files do not store color assignments.

The full STL is one watertight, consistently wound solid with positive volume and Euler characteristic -2. Black and white STLs are watertight, and their union reconstructs the full model without overlapping volumes. Reloaded STL meshes pass Manifold3D checks. The 3MF's serialized meshes are independently verified watertight. The center and tail opening boundaries agree with the original at Z=0.1, 6, and 12 mm within 0.001 mm (middle and upper sections agree to numerical precision). See validation.json.
